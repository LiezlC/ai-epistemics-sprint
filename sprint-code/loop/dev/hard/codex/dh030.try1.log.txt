  volume against veracity) will need it. **Budget roughly one per article, placed where the

- **No triadic or triplet structures.** Avoid three-item lists, three parallel clauses, and chains

- **US spelling** throughout (z not s, -er not -re, -or not -our), despite the SA location,
  unless quoting an external source.
- **Reference prior episodes by title + hyperlink, never by number** (platform episode counts
  diverge; titles + links stay stable).
- **Paragraph flow over dramatic single-line breaks.** Prefer flowing paragraphs, one concept
  each; use single-line beats sparingly for emphasis; headings and bullets fine in moderation.
  The objection is to formulaic templating, not to structure as such.
- **No self-deprecating qualifier tics**. Cut "more than I should," "annoyingly so," "perhaps
  too often," and similar hedges. Tropish AI-prose tell.
- **No autobiography in the content body.** Bio belongs in the bio. Never weave the author's
  personal/professional CV into article, episode, or chapter prose, even as credibility.
- **"We" = the author + her AI cohort**, disclosed and deliberate; never the corporate-plural
  "we." **"Sociable"** carries the sociological sense (mutuality, audit, being addressed back),
  never the community-platform default.
- **Light cross-persona filter:** traces of wonder-permission and a sardonic edge may colour the
  analytical voice; do not fully collapse registers, and do not strip all trace (it makes the
  voice brittle).

## How to run these

Run them as **separate passes**, not as one read-through. Each family hides from whatever pattern
caught the previous one: a scan tuned to em dashes will not see a disguised not-X-but-Y, and a
scan for self-reference will not see self-congratulation, because the latter never uses the word
"arc". Rules 3 to 6 in particular were each found only after the one before it had been cleared.

A narration-read pass (reading aloud or TTS) catches a family the pattern scans miss: sentences
that parse but stumble when spoken, same-word chains, and ornament qualifiers. Liezl's
read-aloud edits are the calibration source for that pass; fold her corrections back into these
rules rather than treating them as one-offs.
